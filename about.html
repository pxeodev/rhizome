About the practice

We build AI systems for a living.

The Rhizome Space is an independent AI consultancy based in Chiang Mai, working online worldwide. We build production AI systems and multi-agent workflows, and we bring that hands-on experience to work professionals and small teams do every day.

No mystery platform and no anonymous hand-off. The person who scopes the work does the testing, the documentation and the handover.

Every engagement is fixed in scope: you know the time, the price and the deliverable in writing before you commit. Work starts from a task you already do, uses sanitized examples until boundaries are agreed, and ends with a documented method that belongs to you.

Production-mindedWe design around real inputs, failure modes and the person accountable for the result.
Tool-independentWe choose around the task and your approved environment, not a software reseller agreement.
DocumentedYou keep the workflow, boundaries, templates and next decision after the engagement ends.
