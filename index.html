Data safety and human judgment

Useful does not mean unsupervised.

A workflow is only successful if the people using it understand its boundaries and can check its output.

  • Sanitize firstUse invented, anonymized or explicitly approved examples while designing and testing.
  • Keep review points visibleDocument who checks the output, what they check and what happens when it is wrong.
  • Use the tools your organization permitsA clever workflow is not useful if it violates company policy or customer trust.

Do not bring confidential data to an intro call.

Employer, customer, health, financial and personal information should only be used when your organization has approved the tool, purpose and handling process. We can map the workflow with sanitized examples first.
